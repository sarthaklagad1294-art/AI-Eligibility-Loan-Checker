# AI Loan Eligibility Checker — Design Brief

## Theme Name
**ClearPath Fintech Glass** — a confident, calm dark-glass interface that turns complex loan decisions into a clear next step.

## Design movement
Editorial fintech dashboard meets premium banking app: high-contrast typography, disciplined data hierarchy, translucent panels, and one electric cyan action color against a midnight canvas.

## Core principles
1. Make the next decision obvious.
2. Show the calculation, not just the verdict.
3. Earn trust with restrained motion, transparent disclaimers, and accessible states.
4. Use whitespace and alignment to make financial detail feel approachable.

## Color philosophy
Midnight navy and ink blue create a calm base. Electric cyan marks actions and focus. Emerald, amber, and red are reserved for outcome semantics. Soft violet and teal glows add depth without competing with data.

## Layout paradigm
A single scrolling product story with a sticky glass navbar, spacious hero, a four-card feature rail, then tool sections in alternating two-column layouts. Results sit beside inputs on desktop and stack below on mobile. The history and how-it-works areas close the loop.

## Signature elements
A shield with a checkmark symbolises confidence. Circular and semicircular gauges make the score legible at a glance. Small uppercase eyebrow labels, thin borders, pill badges, and monospace-ish numeric emphasis make the interface feel instrument-grade.

## Interaction philosophy
Inputs should explain themselves through labels and helper text. Every calculation updates visibly. Buttons provide a soft ripple and lift; cards glow on hover. Motion is short, purposeful, and disabled or reduced for users who prefer less animation.

## Animation
Slow ambient orbs, reveal-on-scroll, gauge fill transitions, count-up numbers, and an AI typing indicator. No animation is required to understand or complete a task.

## Typography system
Inter for all UI and copy. 12px uppercase eyebrow labels with letter spacing. 16–18px body text. 40–64px hero display. Numeric outputs use a tabular, high-weight treatment for scanability.

## Brand essence
**Clarity under pressure.** The product should feel like a reliable financial co-pilot, not a sales funnel.

## Brand voice
Warm, practical, concise, Indian BFSI-aware, and transparent about estimates. Prefer “Here’s what shaped your result” to “You failed.”

## Wordmark / logo
A flat shield silhouette with an internal checkmark and a small rising path cutout. The symbol is drawn with solid cyan and teal geometry, no baked-in text, no gradients, and enough negative space to remain clear at 24px.

## Signature brand color
Electric cyan `#38bdf8`, used for primary actions, focus rings, and the shield-check symbol.
